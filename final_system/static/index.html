📚 Understanding the Multi-Layer Stacking Architecture
• L=2 Bilayers: The ground-truth 2-layer parent seeds determined from initial quantum chemical / DFT simulations.
• L=3 Trilayers: Formed by taking stable L=2 parents and systematically stacking a 3rd monolayer at either the Upper (top) or Lower (bottom) interface across rotation angles θ and displacements t (Å).
• L=4 & L=5 Stacks: Recursively generated by stacking successive monolayers onto verified stable (L-1) parents. All shown structures have negative binding energy (ΔE ≤ 0).
